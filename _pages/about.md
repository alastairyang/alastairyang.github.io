Donglai Yang is a 3rd-year Ph.D. candidate in <a href='https://glacier-geophys.eas.gatech.edu'>Polar Geophysical Simulation Lab</a> at Georgia Institute of Technology and a Polar Informatics Grad Fellow at <a href='https://iharp.umbc.edu/'>iHARP</a>. He is broadly interested in ice sheet dynamic change prediction with numerical modeling, ice-penetrating radar observations, and ML/AI-empowered Bayesian inverse problem to bridge multi-scale geophysical observations and modeling insights.
